RBCD lets back-end service administrators control which front-end accounts can delegate to their services. Traditional constrained delegation uses `msDS-AllowedToDelegateTo` and requires `SeEnableDelegationPrivilege`, typically held by Domain and Enterprise Admins.

RBCD uses the `msDS-AllowedToActOnBehalfOfOtherIdentity` attribute on the back-end service account to specify permitted front-end accounts.

An adversary can leverage RBCD to gain access to any computer if the following conditions are met:
- They have write access to the msDS-AllowedToActOnBehalfOfOtherIdentity attribute of a computer object. 
- They have control of another principal that has an SPN set.
